# Pitch and plan

**Pitch line:** "Every scary letter becomes a 60-second lesson, so people need help less each time."

## Theme fit: Edutainment & Innovation
| Theme word | What FAHAM does | What to show in the video |
|---|---|---|
| Education | "Kenapa saya dapat surat ini?" card after the action plan | A sample letter turned into a lesson card |
| Education | A personal Kamus Surat | The glossary growing after the second letter |
| Entertainment | Spot-the-scam practice mode, scored | A short round |
| Entertainment | A light readiness level (no leaderboard) | A level-up moment |
| Inclusion | Read-aloud plus BM, EN, 中文 and தமிழ் | The same letter explained in two languages |
| Family | "Help my parents" mode | One scan, two languages, sent to a parent |
| Innovation | Plans the route, checks for scams, drafts the reply | Online steps, walk-in checklist and the scam flag |

## 5-minute video
| Time | Scene | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:40 | The problem | A worried person with a letter, and no family member around to help |
| 0:40–1:30 | Read and verify | Scan, classify, extract, scam check; the reasoning appears on screen |
| 1:30–2:30 | Explain and learn | Explanation in the user's language, the "Kenapa?" card and a new glossary entry |
| 2:30–3:30 | Act | Online steps, the walk-in checklist, and the reminder and draft approved by the user |
| 3:30–4:30 | Practice mode | A Spot-the-scam round with a score |
| 4:30–5:00 | Impact | Who it helps, the metric, future scope |

**Target metric:** how often a returning user still needs the agent's full help on a letter type they have seen before. Do not claim a figure until it has been tested.
