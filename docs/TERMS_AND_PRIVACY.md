# Tulen Terms of Use and Privacy Notice

Version of 6 November 2026. These documents are a draft written for a prototype. They may change, and they have not been reviewed by a lawyer.

**In short**

- Tulen helps you spot scam signs in letters and messages and understand what they say. It is guidance, not advice, and it can be wrong.
- A result of "no scam signs found" is not proof that something is real. Always check with the official agency yourself.

### 3. What Tulen does and does not do

## Privacy Notice

This notice explains what happens to information when you use Tulen. It is written to follow the aims of Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010. We do not claim to be certified or registered under it.

### 1. What we handle

- The text, photo, document or email you send for checking.
- The language you choose and, only if you type it, a postcode to find the nearest office.
- If you use email: your email address and your message, so we can reply. If you use Messenger: an ID that Messenger gives us, stored only as a scrambled code.
- On your own device: your language, light or dark choice, voice and easy-mode settings, words you add to your dictionary, your points, your agreement and one small cookie that records that you finished the welcome.

### 2. Why

Only to check what you sent, explain it, and reply to you. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or analytics.

### 3. Hiding personal details first

Before anything goes to an AI or voice service, we hide IC numbers, phone numbers, account numbers, email addresses, addresses, car plates and names. This is done by rules and can miss things. If long number strings remain, the request is not sent.

### 4. What we keep

- Letters, photos and files are read in memory and not saved by us.
- We have no accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
- Our servers keep short technical logs without message content. Counters for limits, and consent for chat channels, are kept in memory as scrambled codes and are lost when the service restarts or after up to 30 days.

### 5. Who else sees it

- AI and voice: masked text goes to ILMU, and to Anthropic if ILMU is unavailable.
- Cloudflare carries web traffic and incoming email to us.
- Mailtrap sends our email replies and keeps delivery logs that include the reply.
- Meta carries Messenger messages.
- Some of these providers may process data outside Malaysia. Their own policies decide how long they keep it.

### 6. Your choices and rights

- You can choose not to send anything, or stop a chat by replying STOP or BERHENTI.
- In About, the button "Clear my data" removes everything Tulen stored on your device.
- You may ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. We hold very little, but we will tell you what we can find. Use the contact in the Terms.

### 7. Security and children

- Traffic uses HTTPS, personal details are hidden first, requests are limited, and logs hold no message content. No system is perfect, so please do not send more than you need to.
- If you are under 18, please use Tulen with a parent or guardian.

### 8. Complaints

Please contact us first. You may also complain to Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Department (www.pdp.gov.my).
