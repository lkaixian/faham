# FAHAM Code of Conduct

Version of 6 November 2026. These documents are a draft written for a prototype. They may change, and they have not been reviewed by a lawyer.



## Code of Conduct

This code applies to everyone who uses FAHAM (the website, embeds, email and Messenger) and to everyone who takes part in the project's repository, issues and discussions. It is not a rulebook to argue over. Treat it in good faith.

### 1. What we ask of you

- Be respectful. Disagree without insults.
- Be honest. Do not pass FAHAM's results off as an official decision, and do not pretend to be an agency or another person.
- Protect other people's information. Do not send or publish someone else's letters, ID details or contact details without their permission.
- Use the service fairly, and stop when asked.

### 2. What is not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, hate or abuse, in any language, including using FAHAM to send them.
- Impersonating an agency, a person, or FAHAM.
- Using FAHAM to create, test or improve scams, or to get scam messages past checks.
- Publishing someone's private information, or tracking or pressuring a person.
- Illegal content.
- Attacking, overloading or scraping the service, or getting around its limits.
- Encouraging others to do any of the above, or carrying on after being asked to stop.
- Making false reports under this code.

### 3. What the maintainers do

Maintainers may remove content, refuse requests and block users who break this code. They will try to be fair, keep reports private, and explain decisions when they can.

### 4. How we respond

- A reminder for a small first slip.
- A warning if it continues or is more serious.
- A temporary block.
- A permanent block, which may be applied straight away for serious harm or technical abuse.

### 5. Reporting and appeals

Report a problem at https://github.com/lkaixian/faham/issues with the title "Conduct report" and no personal details. We will arrange a private way to continue. We will not allow retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith. If you disagree with a decision, write back and tell us what you think we missed.

Found a security problem? Do not post it publicly. Say in an issue that you have a security report and we will arrange a private way to receive it. Please do not access other people's data while testing.

### 6. Where this comes from

This code was written for FAHAM, with ideas drawn from the Contributor Covenant 2.1, the Mozilla Community Participation Guidelines, the Ubuntu Code of Conduct, the Wikimedia Universal Code of Conduct, the Python Software Foundation Code of Conduct and the GitHub Acceptable Use Policies. The wording is our own.
